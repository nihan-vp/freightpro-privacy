1. Introduction

Freight Pro is a freight and logistics marketplace developed by Nihan Ali VP (Freight Pro Logistics Technologies Private Limited). It connects truck drivers, truck owners, transporters and shippers so that a shipper can find a truck for a route and a truck owner can find a load for a return trip.

This policy is published by Freight Pro Logistics Technologies Private Limited, developed by Nihan Ali VP, Freight Pro Logistics Hub, Outer Ring Road, Bellandur, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560103, India ("Freight Pro", "we", "us"). It covers the Freight Pro Android application, the Freight Pro web application and the servers that power them (together, the "Service").

By creating an account you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service. Where Indian law applies, we process personal data on the basis of your consent and to perform the service you have requested, as described in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

The short version: we collect the phone number, profile details, vehicle details and marketplace listings you give us; we show listings to other users; we hide your phone number until you accept a request; we do not run advertising, analytics or location tracking; and you can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app.

3. Personal information

The following categories identify you or can be linked to you, and are treated as personal data:

  • Phone number — the account identifier. It is shown to other users only in limited cases (see section 9).
  • Name, company name, city and district — shown on your public profile and on your listings.
  • Profile photo — shown on your public profile when you add one.
  • Vehicle registration numbers — shown in full to you and to users whose request you have accepted; partly masked for everyone else.
  • Listings, requests and their messages — visible to the users they are addressed to, and listings are visible to anyone using search.
  • Uploaded images — visible wherever the related listing or profile is visible.

Your phone number is not included in public profiles or in public post listings. It is released to the other party only after a load request between you has been accepted, so that you can call each other. You can also choose not to publish a contact preference at all.

4. Device information

The app itself does not read a device identifier. Two limited exceptions exist:

  • Push notifications. If the build you installed uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, the app asks the operating system for notification permission and obtains a device push token from Google. The token identifies the app installation, not you. In the current release the token is only used locally to keep the notification channel alive and is not sent to our servers; if a future release registers it with our API, this policy will be updated first.
  • Network metadata. Like any HTTPS request, calls to our API expose your IP address and standard HTTP headers (user agent, language) to our servers and to our hosting provider for the duration of the request. We use the IP address for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

We do not collect the advertising ID (GAID/IDFA), IMEI, MAC address, installed-app list, or device fingerprint.

5. Analytics and diagnostics

This release contains no analytics, attribution, advertising or crash-reporting SDK. Specifically, it does not include Google Analytics/Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, AppsFlyer, Meta SDK or any ad network.

Operationally, the API records:

  • error logs for server-side failures (HTTP 5xx), which may contain a request path and an error stack trace but no request body or password;
  • rate-limit counters derived from the IP address;
  • SMS delivery logs in which phone numbers are masked (only the last three digits are stored in the log line).

These logs are used for debugging and security only, are not used for profiling or advertising, and are retained for 30 days.

6. Cookies and tracking technologies

  • Android app: no cookies and no web beacons. A small amount of data is kept in the app's private storage (SharedPreferences): your session tokens, your cached profile, your language and notification preferences, bookmark state and an unsent post draft. This data is sandboxed to the app, excluded from Android cloud backup in this release, and cleared when you log out or delete the account.
  • Web build: no cookies and no third-party trackers. The same session and preferences are stored in the browser's localStorage, which is cleared when you log out or clear site data.
  • This policy page: sets no cookies and loads no external resources — all styling is embedded in the file.

We do not use cookies or local storage for advertising, cross-site tracking or profiling.

8. Data storage and security

Data is stored on servers operated by MongoDB Atlas & Cloud Infrastructure in the Asia-Pacific (Mumbai, India) region. Cross-border transfers, if any, rely on statutory adequacy standards and contractual safeguards compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Security measures in the shipped product and API include:

  • HTTPS-only communication; the release build blocks plaintext HTTP and does not trust user-installed certificate authorities.
  • Short-lived access tokens (15 minutes by default) with rotating refresh tokens; failed refreshes end the session.
  • OTPs are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, expire after a few minutes, allow a limited number of attempts, and are deleted after use or expiry.
  • Authorisation checks on the server: you can only edit or delete your own profile, posts, vehicles and requests. Phone numbers are withheld on public endpoints until a request is accepted.
  • Image uploads restricted by type and size, served from a separate path with content-type protection.
  • Hardened API configuration: security headers, request-size limits, per-IP rate limiting, an explicit CORS allow-list in production, and a MongoDB instance that is not exposed to the internet.
  • The production container runs as a non-root user.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by applicable law.

9. Data sharing

We share personal data only in these situations:

  • With other users, to run the marketplace. Your name, user type, company name, city, profile photo and the content of your listings are public to signed-in users. Your phone number becomes visible to the other party once a load request between you is accepted. Vehicle registration numbers are shown masked to users who are not party to an accepted request.
  • With service providers listed in section 7, strictly to operate the Service.
  • For legal reasons — if we are required to by law, a court order or a lawful request from a competent authority, or to protect the rights, safety and property of users, the public or Freight Pro.
  • In a business transfer — if the Service is merged, acquired or its assets transferred, data may move to the successor, subject to this policy.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. There are no advertising SDKs in the app.

10. Data retention

  • Account and profile data — kept while your account exists.
  • OTP records — deleted automatically within minutes of expiry, and immediately after successful verification.
  • Listings, requests, vehicles, saved items and uploaded images — kept until you delete them or delete your account. Completed or cancelled posts remain visible to the parties involved until removed.
  • After account deletion — your profile, listings, vehicles, requests, saved items, uploaded images and OTP records are deleted from the live database. Residual copies in encrypted backups expire within 30 days.
  • Logs and security records — retained for 30 days for debugging and abuse prevention.
  • Records we must keep by law — for example tax or lawful-request records, retained for the period required by law even after deletion.

11. Your rights and data deletion

Your rights

  • Access and portability — see the profile, listings and requests in the app, or ask us for a copy of your data.
  • Correction — edit your name, company, city, district, language and photo in Profile → Edit profile; edit or delete vehicles and listings at any time.
  • Deletion — delete individual items, or delete your whole account (see below).
  • Withdraw consent — turn off notifications in Settings, or delete your account. Withdrawing consent for essential processing means you can no longer use the Service.
  • Grievance redressal — contact our grievance officer (section 14). We respond within the period required by applicable law.
  • Complain to a regulator — you may lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India or another competent authority.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the screen and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent: your profile, truck posts (including return trips), vehicles, requests you sent or received, requests on your posts, saved items, uploaded images and OTP records are removed, and you are signed out. You can create a new account later with the same phone number, but the old data cannot be restored.

If you cannot sign in, you can request deletion by emailing privacy@freightpro.com from the phone number in question; we may ask you to verify ownership of the number first. The same instructions are published at https://fp.livetools.me/account-deletion.html.

12. Children's privacy

Freight Pro is a commercial transport marketplace intended for people who are at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into contracts. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at privacy@freightpro.com and we will delete the account and its data.
